Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?
Open Veritas → Settings tab → Support → "Restore purchase". You can also tap "Restore purchase" at the bottom of the Premium screen. Have a promo code? Use "Redeem a code" in the same place.
My quote hasn't changed today — is that a bug?
Quotes rotate daily based on the date. If you're seeing the same quote, check that your device date is set correctly in Settings → General → Date & Time.
How do I add the widget to my home screen?
Long press your home screen until icons jiggle → tap the + button → search "Veritas" → choose a size and tap Add Widget.
How do I choose which thinkers my daily quote comes from?
Open Veritas → Settings tab → "Your daily quote" → Rooms, and pick any of the seven rooms (the Stoa, the Academy, the Eastern Masters and so on). Your daily quote, widget and reminder all follow the rooms you pick.
Will my saved quotes transfer to a new iPhone?
Yes — if iCloud is enabled on both devices, your saved quotes, notes and reflections sync automatically via iCloud.
How do I delete my data?
Unsave a quote by tapping its heart, and delete a reflection from its page with "Delete reflection". Deletions sync to your other devices. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.
How do I cancel my Premium subscription?
Veritas Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There is nothing to cancel.
